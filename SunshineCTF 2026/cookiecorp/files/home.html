How it works

Bakers fabricate custom cookie recipes in the Fabricator. When a batch is ready, submit it for review — our automated Quality Inspector loads your recipe into the fabrication mixer and stamps a verdict. Approved batches receive an official seal.

Note: only the CookieCorp Chief can award the coveted Golden Seal. Ordinary inspectors may only apply the standard seal.
