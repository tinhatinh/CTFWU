Cloudy with a Chance of Spaceships ☁️🚀

The fleet lives in the cloud now. Every craft phones home to the ground station to report how it's doing.

Space runs hot and cold, so the hull temperature swings around depending on where a craft is parked. Nothing a good forecast can't handle.

To save you squinting at the sky, we've wired up a handy board of live hull readings for the fleet below. Pick a ship and we'll take its temperature!
