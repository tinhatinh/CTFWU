NoiseGate Buds :: BLE sniff

We sniffed a pair of NoiseGate wireless earbuds coming up and talking to their companion app over Bluetooth Low Energy. The capture is a standard BLE link-layer recording.

  • file: capture.pcap (open in Wireshark: it dissects btle / btatt natively)

There is more than one device in the air. Two secrets are in here: one the earbuds leak in a config read, and one they only hand over after the app authenticates.
