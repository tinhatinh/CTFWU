About

Independent since 2016

Tapedeck started because the tools for hosting a show were either bloated or built to lock creators in. We wanted something calm and dependable that treats the feed as yours.

Today a small team in Providence keeps the lights on for fourteen thousand shows, from kitchen-table hobbies to newsroom dailies. We are self-funded and plan to stay that way.

We build slowly and on purpose. If a feature does not help you make or grow the show, it does not ship.
