skill still wins

somewhere along the way, jeopardy ctf became a contest of whose AI budget is bigger, not who can actually hack. h7ctf ’26 puts the human back on top.

we don't ban AI; you can't, online. instead the quals run on a scoring economy where judgment beats throughput, so no stack of agents can buy the top of the board. the onsite finals are human-only. AI's welcome as a tool. it just doesn't get to win for you.
