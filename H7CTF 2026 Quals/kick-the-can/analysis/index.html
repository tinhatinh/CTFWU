Zero-Day Highway :: CAN bus capture

We tapped the CAN bus of a connected vehicle through its OBD-II port during a service session. Most of the traffic is routine powertrain chatter, but a diagnostic tool was talking to an ECU at the same time, and the ECU handed back something it should not have.

  • file: capture.log (SocketCAN candump log: (timestamp) can0 ID#DATA)

Read it with candump/can-utils, Wireshark (SocketCAN), or python-can. Recover what the ECU leaked.
